After the customers go home

The admin work that steals your evenings and weekends

Chasing a client who is 45 days late. Sorting a shoebox of receipts before the bookkeeper's deadline. Reading forty applications for one opening. Sending the same new-hire paperwork for the tenth time this year. It is necessary work, and it usually happens at the kitchen table after hours.

Prepare, organize, hand off

What an AI bookkeeping assistant does, and what it does not

We are careful about this line because money and people are involved. These agents do the gathering, sorting, matching and drafting. Final decisions, filings and advice stay with the owner and the professionals you already trust.

Fair by design

AI HR screening that stays fair, consistent and human-decided

Hiring is where we are most cautious. According to SHRM's 2024 survey of 2,366 HR professionals, about one in four employers use AI to support HR activities, and among those using it for recruiting, roughly a third use it to review or screen resumes. Respondents also named the fear of losing the "human touch" as a reason to hold back. We agree that concern deserves respect.

So the Applicant Screening Agent is built around a few fixed rules:

  1. You write the criteria. Only job-related requirements, such as licenses, availability or specific experience, that you define in writing before launch.
  2. Every applicant gets the same treatment. Same questions, same summary format, same criteria, in the same order.
  3. No rejections by the agent. It organizes and summarizes; a person reviews every application and makes every advance or decline decision.
  4. No protected characteristics. The agent is instructed not to consider or infer age, race, gender, disability or any other protected trait, and we review its summaries for that during testing.
  5. Local rules first. Some states and cities regulate automated hiring tools. We recommend checking with your employment attorney, and we configure notices or audits as they advise.

Where to begin

Picking your first admin agent

You do not need the whole squad. Most owners start with whichever task causes the most dread on Sunday night. A few honest rules of thumb:

If cash flow keeps you up

Start with the Invoice Reminder Agent. It is a Scout, so it is one of the lower-cost builds, and it works on the money that is already yours. Reminders are written in your tone and stop the moment a payment posts.

If month-end is chaos

Pair the Receipt & Expense Agent with Bookkeeping Prep. Receipts get matched as they arrive, so the package your bookkeeper receives is already organized and the hours they bill go to review instead of sorting.

If you are hiring this quarter

Applicant Screening and Onboarding work well together. One keeps a flood of applications organized for a fair human review; the other makes sure the person you choose has a smooth first week.

Each agent is designed to be narrow. The invoice agent does not touch payroll. The screening agent does not see your bank feed. Keeping jobs separate keeps access tight and makes every agent easier to test, audit and adjust. If you later add several, a Commander Agent can gather their approval requests into one daily queue so you are not answering five separate notifications.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can an AI bookkeeping assistant replace my bookkeeper or CPA?

No. The Bookkeeping Prep Agent suggests categories, matches receipts and assembles a month-end package. Your bookkeeper or CPA reviews, adjusts and closes the books. The agents do not give tax, accounting or legal advice.

How does AI HR screening stay fair?

You write job-related criteria before launch, every applicant is summarized the same way, the agent is instructed not to consider protected characteristics, and a person reviews every application and makes every decision. We also recommend checking local rules on automated hiring tools with your employment attorney.

Will the Applicant Screening Agent reject candidates?

No. It organizes and summarizes applications against your criteria and schedules interviews for the candidates you choose. Every advance or decline decision is made by a person.

What accounting and HR tools do these agents connect to?

Common connections include QuickBooks Online, Xero, FreshBooks, Stripe, Square, Gusto, ADP, BambooHR, Indeed, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox and e-signature tools. Access is limited to what each job needs.
