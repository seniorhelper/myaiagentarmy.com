FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of AI agents can you build?

We build agents across seven squads: receptionist, customer service, sales, marketing, operations, admin and finance, and personal life. If you need a job that is not on the roster, we can usually design a custom agent for it during the Blueprint.

What do Scout, Specialist and Commander mean?

They are agent ranks. A Scout handles one task with one or two tools, a Specialist runs a multi-step workflow across several tools, and a Commander coordinates other agents and reports to you.

Which AI agent should a small business start with?

Most start with the job that interrupts them most, often answering calls with an AI Receptionist or responding to new leads with a Speed-to-Lead Responder.

Can I combine agents from different squads?

Yes. Agents from any squad can work together, passing work between them, and a Commander Agent can coordinate larger teams.
