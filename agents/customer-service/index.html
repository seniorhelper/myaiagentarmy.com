Where support hours go

Your support team answers the same twenty questions all day

"Where is my order?" "Can I change my appointment?" "How do I return this?" Each one takes two minutes. Multiply by a few hundred a week and someone on your team has a full-time job typing the same replies.

Meanwhile, the customers with real problems wait in the same queue as the ones asking for a tracking number. The inbox never feels finished. Your best support people spend their energy on copy and paste instead of on the conversations that actually keep customers loyal.

That repetitive layer is exactly what an AI customer service agent is built for. Not to replace your team. To clear the routine so the people on your team can do the part only people can do.

Where this is heading

Analysts expect agents to handle most routine support, and customers still want people

Gartner predicts that by 2029, agentic AI will autonomously resolve 80% of common customer service issues without human intervention, leading to a 30% reduction in operational costs. That is a forecast, not a guarantee, and the word "common" is doing a lot of work.

The other side of the picture matters just as much. In a Gartner survey of 5,728 customers, 64% said they would prefer companies didn't use AI for customer service, and the top worry was that it would get harder to reach a person. And in a separate Gartner poll, 95% of customer service leaders said they plan to keep human agents to define AI's role.

We design around both facts. Agents answer what they can answer well, quickly and accurately. A person is always one step away. That is how you get the speed of automation without becoming the company nobody can reach.

The human line

What stays human in AI for customer support

We draw this line with you before anything launches, and we write it into the agent's instructions.

  • Anything emotional. A frustrated, grieving or worried customer goes to a person. The agent recognizes the signals and hands off with the full conversation attached so nobody has to repeat themselves.
  • Anything that costs money. Refunds, credits, discounts and replacements are drafted by the agent and approved by your team.
  • Anything new. Questions not covered by your approved content get "let me get someone who can help," never an improvised answer.
  • Anyone who asks. "Can I talk to a person?" always works. Making that easy is how you keep the trust the Gartner research says customers worry about losing.
Heads up: the agent is only as good as the answers it is given. Part of every build is tightening your help content, return policy and canned replies so the agent has a clean source of truth.

Rollout

How we launch AI support without upsetting customers

The fastest way to lose trust is to switch on a bot that guesses. We roll out support agents in stages so your customers only ever see an agent that has proven itself.

Stage 1: Shadow

The agent reads real tickets and drafts replies that only your team sees. You compare its drafts with what your people actually sent and correct it.

Stage 2: Assist

Your team sends the agent's drafts with one click when they are right and edits them when they are not. Triage and tagging go live here.

Stage 3: Answer

For the narrow set of questions it has handled well, such as tracking or store hours, the agent replies directly. Everything else keeps the human step.

You decide when to move from one stage to the next, and you can move back at any time. Many businesses stay in the assist stage for certain topics permanently, and that is a perfectly good outcome. The goal is hours back for your team, not removing people from the conversation.

We also look at which questions keep coming in. If the same confusion shows up fifty times a week, the better fix might be a clearer product page or shipping notice, and the agent's reports make that pattern easy to see. Fewer questions is a win too.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI customer service agent?

It is an AI agent connected to your help desk, inbox, store or shipping tools that handles one support job, such as answering common questions, sorting tickets, looking up orders or preparing returns. It works from your approved content and hands off anything outside it to a person.

Will customers still be able to reach a human?

Yes. Every support agent we build makes it easy to reach a person. Emotional situations, new questions and any request to talk to someone are handed to your team with the full conversation attached.

Can an AI call center agent issue refunds?

It can prepare them. The Returns & Exchanges Agent checks your policy, collects details and drafts the refund or label, but a team member approves anything that spends money.

Which help desk and ecommerce tools does it work with?

Common connections include Zendesk, Freshdesk, Help Scout, Gorgias, Gmail, Outlook, Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, ShipStation, Slack and Microsoft Teams. Your exact setup is mapped during the Agent Blueprint.
