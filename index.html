How the factory works

From "I'm drowning" to a working agent, in four moves

  1. Blueprint. We map where your hours go, pick the agents that give the most time back first, and write it all down: jobs, tools, guardrails, timeline.
  2. Build. Our developers build and test each agent against your real situations before it ever talks to a customer.
  3. Deploy in waves. Agents go live one wave at a time, starting with the safest, biggest wins. Deployment is scoped once we've seen your setup.
  4. Care. We monitor, tune and update your agents as your business changes, so they get sharper instead of stale.

Agents do the work. You stay the boss.

An AI agent that can act needs rules, so every agent we build gets the same chain of command: one clear job, the least access it needs, a log of everything it does, and a human approval step on anything that spends money, makes a promise or says something sensitive.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy just one AI agent?

Yes. Many people start with a single agent, most often an AI Receptionist or a Speed-to-Lead Responder, and add more once they see the hours come back. You never have to buy a whole army.

Is ordering up to 100 AI agents realistic?

Yes, when each agent does one narrow job and the army is deployed in waves. Larger orders usually make sense for multi-location businesses with many repeatable workflows. We start with the agents that free up the most time fastest and measure each wave before adding the next.

What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions. An AI agent works toward a goal: it can check a calendar, book an appointment, draft a quote, send a follow-up and report back, within rules and approval steps you set.

What do AI agents cost?

Build and Own pricing starts at $1,500 per agent for a Scout, $3,500 for a Specialist and $7,500 for a Commander, with optional Agent Care from $99 per month. Subscribe-to-Own starts at $149, $299 or $599 per agent per month with Care included. Deployment is scoped after your Agent Blueprint.

Who handles deployment?

You choose your level of help: Done-For-You, where we deploy and run your agents; Co-Pilot, where your team deploys with our guidance; or Handoff, where you get configured agents with documentation. Deployment is priced after the Blueprint because every business's tools are different.

Will AI agents replace my team?

They are designed to take repetitive work off your team's plate, not replace the people who build relationships, make judgment calls and close deals. Most owners use the time back to grow, serve customers better or simply work fewer nights.
