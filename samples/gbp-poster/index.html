How these get published

Google Business Profile is not connected, so these are copy-and-paste drafts. Open your Business Profile, choose Add update, paste the text, add a photo if you like, and pick the button shown above. The CSV in this folder has every post in one place. Approve a draft with `army approve <id> --client <client>`. Approved posts publish to Google the next time `army send` runs (the scheduler runs it), so approve each post on or just before its suggested day. The 'Post on' date is a suggestion; Google does not hold posts for later.

Rules every post follows

  • No phone numbers, emails or links in the text (Google may reject them). The button handles calls and links.
  • Under 1500 characters; aiming for 100-300 words.
  • Recent-job posts never name the customer or give an address. City at most.
  • Offers appear only when you set them up in the settings. Nothing is invented.
  • Topics rotate so the same kind of post does not repeat until each type has had a turn.
