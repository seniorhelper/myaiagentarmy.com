About this audit

Agent readiness is an emerging area. AI companies change their crawlers and how they use websites often, and standards such as llms.txt and agent descriptor files are proposals that not every AI tool reads. These checks are current best practices that make your business easier for assistants to find, understand and contact. They do not guarantee that any AI tool will recommend you, or any ranking or traffic.
