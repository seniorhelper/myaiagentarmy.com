How this is scored

Each source is checked field by field (phone and address count most, then name, then website and hours). A formatting difference such as 'St' vs 'Street' or '(303) 555-0142' vs '303.555.0142' counts as a match. A blank field earns half credit, a conflict none, and a missing listing none. Google, Apple, Bing, Yelp and your own website weigh more than smaller directories.

About fixing listings

Most directories have no public API for updating a listing, so these fixes are made by hand in each site's business dashboard (or through your listing management service). Changes can take days to weeks to show. Values in the listings file are only as current as your last export.
