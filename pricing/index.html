Why it works this way

Reusable templates are why ready-made costs less

Because a ready-made agent starts from a template we've already engineered, you pay for setup and testing, not invention. That's how an AI Receptionist can start at $3,500 to own instead of a five-figure custom build.

And when you need something truly yours, custom builds give you full ownership of the custom work. Many businesses mix both: ready-made agents for the common jobs, one custom agent for the thing that makes them different.

Your data never travels. We never reuse a client's data, settings or anything specific to their business. What we reuse is the general engineering, the same way a builder reuses blueprints, not your furniture.

Why we quote development, and scope deployment separately

Building an agent is something we can price up front. Plugging it into your world is different.

Development: priced up front

Designing the agent's job, writing its instructions and rules, building its tools and testing it against real scenarios. This is what the starting prices and the Factory estimate cover.

Deployment: scoped after the Blueprint

Connecting your phone system, CRM, inbox, calendar and payment tools, setting permissions, cleaning data and training your team. One business has three tools; another has fourteen. You get a written scope and price before anything starts.

Run the math

Own it vs. Subscribe-to-Own over time

One ready-made Specialist agent at starting prices: owning it with Agent Care versus Subscribe-to-Own. Lower upfront cost or lower long-run cost. Pick what fits your cash flow.

Squad volume pricing

Agents that share tools, data and setup cost less to build together. Estimated savings on ready-made build and setup fees: 10% at 5–9 agents, 15% at 10–24, 20% at 25+. Monthly rates see 5%, 10% and 15%.

Copies scale cheaply

The same agent for another location or team? Extra copies of an agent you already have are estimated at 50% of the build or setup fee, or 70% of the monthly rate. Franchises and multi-location teams can run thousands of copies, rolled out in waves.

Usage, at cost

AI model usage, phone minutes and third-party software are billed at cost or run on your own accounts. No markup games. Your Blueprint estimates them for your volume.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does an AI agent cost?

Ready-made agents start at $1,500 to own for a Scout, $3,500 for a Specialist and $7,500 for a Commander. On Subscribe-to-Own they start at $149, $299 or $599 per month plus a one-time setup fee of $500, $1,000 or $1,500. Custom-built agents start at $4,000 for a single workflow.

What is the difference between ready-made and custom-built agents?

Ready-made agents start from our agent templates, then get set up with your business details, rules and tools and tested on your real situations, so they launch faster and cost less. Custom-built agents are designed and coded from scratch by our full-stack developers for workflows that don't fit a template.

Do I own my AI agent?

Yes. You own your agent's setup, all of your data and everything it produces, with a permanent license to run it. On custom builds, the custom code written for you is yours outright. We keep only our general engine and reusable templates.

Will you reuse my agent for other businesses?

We reuse our general engine and templates, which is what keeps ready-made agents affordable. We never reuse your data, your settings or anything specific to your business.

What is Subscribe-to-Own?

Instead of a large build fee, you pay a small one-time setup fee and a monthly price per agent for a 2 to 4 year term, with monitoring, tuning and updates included. You then have the option to buy the agent out at fair market value.

Why is deployment priced separately?

Deployment depends on your phone system, CRM, inbox, calendar, permissions and data, and that varies widely between businesses. We scope it during the Agent Blueprint and give you a written price before any work starts.

Are there ongoing usage costs?

Yes. AI model usage, phone minutes and third-party software are billed at cost or run on your own accounts. Your Blueprint estimates these based on your expected volume.
