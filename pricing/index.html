The honest part

Why we quote development, and scope deployment separately

Building an agent is something we can price up front. Plugging it into your world is different.

Development: priced up front

Designing the agent's job, writing its instructions and rules, building its tools and testing it against real scenarios. This is what the starting prices and the Factory estimate cover.

Deployment: scoped after the Blueprint

Connecting your phone system, CRM, inbox, calendar and payment tools, setting permissions, cleaning data and training your team. One business has three tools; another has fourteen. You get a written scope and price before anything starts.

Squad volume pricing

Agents that share tools, data and setup cost less to build together. Estimated build savings: 10% at 5–9 agents, 15% at 10–24, 20% at 25+. Subscriptions see 5%, 10% and 15%.

Extra copies

Need the same agent for another location or team? Additional copies of an agent you already own are estimated at 50% of the build fee, or 70% of the monthly rate.

Usage, at cost

AI model usage, phone minutes and third-party software are billed at cost or run on your own accounts. No markup games. The Blueprint estimates these for your volume.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a custom AI agent cost?

Build and Own starts at $1,500 for a Scout, $3,500 for a Specialist and $7,500 for a Commander, as a one-time development fee per agent. Subscribe-to-Own starts at $149, $299 or $599 per agent per month with Agent Care included.

What is Subscribe-to-Own?

Instead of a large build fee, you pay a monthly price per agent for a 2 to 4 year term, with monitoring, tuning and updates included. You then have the option to buy the agent out at fair market value.

What does Agent Care include?

Agent Care includes monitoring, tuning instructions and rules as your business changes, and updating agents when tools or AI models change. It starts at $99 per agent per month on Build and Own and is included with Subscribe-to-Own.

Why is deployment priced separately?

Deployment depends on your phone system, CRM, inbox, calendar, permissions and data, and that varies widely between businesses. We scope it during the Agent Blueprint and give you a written price before any work starts.

Are there ongoing usage costs?

Yes. AI model usage, phone minutes and third-party software are billed at cost or run on your own accounts. Your Blueprint estimates these based on your expected volume.

Do you offer discounts for multiple agents?

Yes. Squads of five or more get estimated volume pricing because agents share tools, data and setup, and extra copies of an agent you already own cost less to build.
