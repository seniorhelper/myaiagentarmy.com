Who's behind the factory

Built by Eye To Ad Media, headquartered in Denver

My AI Agent Army is the AI agent division of Eye To Ad Media, a Denver-headquartered team that has been building websites, software and marketing systems for businesses since 2012. We grow businesses all over the world, from local service companies to national brands.

Our founder and CEO, Zach Wennstedt, started down the marketing path in 2011. Before that he managed sales teams for multi-million-dollar plumbing companies and ran multi-million-dollar national remodeling companies. That's where the idea behind this factory comes from: he's lived the missed calls, the leads that went cold, the quotes written at midnight, and the feeling that the business was running him instead of the other way around.

Why agents, and why now

For years the only way to get more done was to hire more people or work more hours. AI agents add a third option: hand the repetitive, predictable work to software that does it well and checks with you on what matters. We think that's one of the biggest gifts technology can give a business owner. Not more features, but more time.

We also think most people don't yet know what an agent can do for them, so we explain things in plain English, show the real numbers, and start small.

Frequently asked questions

Who builds the AI agents?

No. We are headquartered in Denver and work with businesses all over the world.

No one can honestly guarantee results with AI or marketing, because technology and markets change constantly. We promise to treat your business like our own, build agents that do real work, and keep improving them.
