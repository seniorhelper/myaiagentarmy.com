October tip from Sunrise Plumbing: get ready for the first freeze

Disconnect garden hoses, shut off and drain outdoor faucets, and blow out sprinkler lines before overnight lows drop below freezing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are these real agent results or a mockup?

They are real. Every report and draft on this page was produced by our agents running on a sample business with sample data: a sample inbox, calendar, customer list, invoices, bank exports and a sample website. We did not edit the output. The business and its customers are made up, so no real person's information is shown.

How were the agents tested?

Each agent has automated tests that check its numbers, its edge cases and its safety rules, more than 300 tests in all, run on three versions of Python. The tests also confirm that agents never send anything without approval, never create duplicate drafts, and still work when the AI model is unavailable.

Do these agents need an AI model to work?

No. Every agent has a built-in path that works with no AI model at all, which is what you see on this page. With an AI model connected, wording and judgment improve, and anything the AI writes is checked for invented prices, phone numbers, links or facts before it is used.

Will my agents send emails or post without asking me?

No. Agents draft. You approve. Emails, texts and posts wait in an outbox until you approve them, unless you choose to let a specific kind skip approval, such as the morning briefing sent to yourself. Every action is written to an audit log.

Can these agents be set up for my business?

Yes. These are our ready-made agents. During the Agent Blueprint we connect them to your inbox, calendar, files and accounts, adjust them to how you work, and test them on your real situations before launch.
