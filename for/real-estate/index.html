AI for real estate agents who would rather be with clients

Real estate is a relationship business that happens to be buried in logistics. Showing requests at 10 p.m., portal leads that arrive while you are mid-listing appointment, transaction deadlines spread across five inboxes. AI agents for real estate are built to carry that logistics load so you can spend your hours where clients actually feel them: at the kitchen table, at the open house, across the negotiating table.

Each agent we build has one narrow job, plugs into the systems your brokerage already runs (your CRM, MLS-fed website forms, calendar, e-signature and transaction tools) and passes anything that calls for professional judgment straight to you.

One lead, start to finish

How a late-night inquiry becomes a scheduled showing

The agent shares only facts you have already published about the property, such as square footage, HOA fees or showing instructions. Questions about value, offers, neighborhoods or anything that drifts into advice are handed to you with the conversation attached.

Fair housing comes first

Built to treat every inquiry the same way

The Fair Housing Act prohibits discrimination based on race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status and disability, and many states and cities add more protected classes. Automation does not get a pass on that. Our real estate agents are designed around a simple principle: every person gets the same process.

No steering

Agents don't describe neighborhoods in terms of who lives there, schools as a proxy for demographics, or anything that nudges someone toward or away from an area. Those questions go to a licensed human.

No unfair screening

Agents don't rank, filter or deprioritize people based on protected characteristics or proxies for them. Qualifying questions are the same for everyone and limited to what you approve.

Scripts you review

You approve the questions, answers and handoff rules before launch, and we recommend your broker or compliance contact review them too.

Logged conversations

Every exchange is recorded in your systems so you can audit what was said and fix anything that needs tightening.

Important: agents support your compliance practices; they don't replace them. You and your brokerage remain responsible for fair housing compliance. Read more about our approval steps and data handling.

Under contract

Keeping a transaction on the rails

Between accepted offer and closing, the work is mostly chasing: documents, signatures and dates. A typical setup looks like this:

  1. Collect. The Document Intake Agent requests the files on your checklist from the right parties and files them in the right folder.
  2. Track. The Daily Briefing Agent sends you a morning list of upcoming contingency dates and what is still missing.
  3. Nudge. Polite reminders go to the people who owe something, in wording you approved.
  4. Escalate. Anything that looks off, such as a missing signature two days from a deadline, lands with you, not in a reminder loop.

The long game

Nurturing the buyer who is a year away

Some of your best future clients are people who are not ready yet. A family waiting for the school year to end. A renter saving for a down payment. A seller who wants to see what the spring market does. These relationships are won by steady, thoughtful contact over months, and that is exactly the kind of contact that falls apart during a busy closing season.

The Follow-Up Agent is designed to keep those conversations alive in your voice. You decide the cadence, the topics and the boundaries. A typical setup might include:

Market check-ins

Short notes when new listings match the criteria a prospect gave you, using only published listing information.

Life-timeline nudges

A friendly touch at the moment they told you they would be ready, such as after a lease ends or a job starts.

Past-client care

Home anniversary notes and reminders that keep you top of mind for referrals, sent only to people who want to hear from you.

Each message respects opt-outs, and any reply that signals real intent ("we're ready to look this weekend") is handed straight to you with the full history, so you can pick up the phone while the moment is warm. The agent keeps the relationship warm; you close the distance.

The same principle applies to listings. The Content Drafting Agent can turn your property notes and photos into a first-draft description and a few social captions, and the Social Posting Agent can schedule open-house reminders. You review every word before it goes public, which matters for accuracy and for fair housing language in listing copy.

The license stays with you

What stays human in a real estate business

Agents handle

  • Fast first replies and approved qualifying questions
  • Scheduling showings and calls
  • Sharing published listing facts
  • Collecting documents and tracking dates
  • Drafting listing descriptions for your edit

You handle

  • Pricing advice and comparative market analysis
  • Offers, counteroffers and every negotiation
  • Neighborhood guidance and fair housing judgment
  • Anything contractual, legal or financial
  • The relationship: trust, reassurance, the hard conversations

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What can AI agents do for real estate agents?

They can reply to portal and website leads quickly, ask the qualifying questions you approve, schedule showings, keep long-term prospects warm, collect transaction documents, track contingency dates and keep your CRM tidy.

How do you handle fair housing with AI agents?

Agents are designed to follow the same process for every inquiry, never steer people toward or away from areas, and never screen based on protected characteristics or proxies for them. You approve the scripts, conversations are logged, and neighborhood or advice questions go to a licensed human. You and your brokerage remain responsible for compliance.

Will the agent negotiate or give pricing advice?

No. Pricing guidance, offers, counteroffers and negotiations stay with you. Agents share only published listing facts and hand everything else to a person.

Does it connect to my real estate CRM?

Agents are built to connect to the CRM, calendar, website forms and transaction tools you already use. Each connection is scoped in writing after the Agent Blueprint because setups vary.
