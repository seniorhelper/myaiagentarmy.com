The heart of it

A personal AI agent is about one thing: more of your life back

Nobody dreams about renewing a car registration, comparing four flight itineraries or untangling who is driving whom to practice on Thursday. Those tasks are small, but they never stop, and they quietly crowd out the people and passions you actually care about. A personal AI agent is built to take that life admin off your plate, so the evening goes to dinner with your family, the weekend goes to the trail, and the morning goes to whatever lights you up.

The idea behind this whole factory started here. Businesses use agents to grow. People use them to breathe. The same narrow, carefully supervised design that runs a company's front desk can quietly keep a household on track.

Household activities include cooking, cleaning and yard work that an agent can't do for you. But mixed in with them is a layer of planning and paperwork that an agent can help with:

  • Bills, autopays and subscriptions you forgot you still have.
  • Renewals: passports, licenses, insurance, warranties, memberships.
  • Schedules for school, sports, appointments and visits that live in five group chats.
  • Trip planning that eats an entire weekend of tabs.
  • Research for big decisions, from a new mattress to a new car.

Privacy first

Your life, your data, your approval

Inviting an agent into your inbox or calendar is personal. We design life agents around a few firm principles:

  1. You approve anything that spends money. Bookings, purchases, payments and cancellations always wait for a yes from you.
  2. Least access. Each agent connects only to what its job requires. The travel agent doesn't need your bank; the calendar agent doesn't need your medical records.
  3. Your accounts where possible. Agents can run on your own email, calendar and AI accounts, so your information stays in services you already chose.
  4. Nothing sent in your name without permission. Drafts are drafts until you say otherwise, unless you set a specific rule, like auto-confirming a dentist appointment.
  5. Easy off switch. Disconnect an agent and its access ends.

The details of how we handle permissions and data are on the safety and privacy page.

What to do with the time

Time back is the whole point

It is easy to talk about agents in terms of tasks completed. That misses why anyone wants one. The real measure is what fills the space they open up. Maybe it is a standing Tuesday dinner that no longer gets canceled. A hobby that has been waiting in the garage for three years. An hour of reading before bed instead of an hour of inbox. A Saturday morning that starts at the farmers market instead of at the kitchen table with a stack of bills.

We built this factory on that idea. Eye To Ad Media has spent years helping businesses grow, and the same lesson keeps coming back: the people running those businesses also have lives, and the busywork follows them home. A personal agent is our way of handing some of those hours back.

People

Show up for the game, the call with your parents, the friend who needs an afternoon.

Passions

Paint, write, build, train, cook. Whatever you keep saying you'll get to someday.

Rest

Unscheduled time counts too. Not every reclaimed hour needs a plan.

Still yours

What stays human in your life

Every decision that matters, which is most of them. Agents don't choose where you go on vacation, how you spend your money, what your family does on Saturday or what you say to people you love. They don't give medical, legal or financial advice. They gather, remind, organize and draft. You live. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a personal AI agent?

It is a small AI helper built to do one personal job well, such as tracking bills and renewals, keeping a family calendar, researching trips or sorting important email. It connects only to the accounts its job needs and checks with you before doing anything that matters.

Will a personal AI agent spend my money?

Not without your approval. Bookings, purchases, payments and cancellations always wait for you to say yes.

Is my personal data safe?

Agents are designed with least access, so each one connects only to what its job requires, and they can run on your own email, calendar and AI accounts. You can disconnect an agent at any time to end its access.

Can it book my travel?

The Travel Planning Agent researches options around your preferences and budget and lays them out for you to compare. You choose and you book, or you approve a specific booking before it happens.

How much does a personal AI assistant agent cost?

Each life agent is a Scout, starting from $1,500 to build and own or from $149 per month plus setup on Subscribe-to-Own with care included. AI usage is billed at cost or runs on your own accounts.
