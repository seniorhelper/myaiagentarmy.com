Built for the trades

AI agents for contractors who are tired of losing jobs to a ringing phone

Plumbers, roofers, HVAC techs, painters, remodelers, landscapers, electricians: your best work happens on a ladder, under a sink or inside a crawlspace. That is exactly when the phone rings, the web form fills up and a homeowner decides to call the next company on the list. An AI agent for your home service business is designed to cover those moments so the crew keeps working and the lead still gets handled.

Every agent we build does one narrow job, connects to the tools you already use (your phone line, your field service software, your calendar, QuickBooks) and hands anything that needs judgment back to a person. You can start with a single AI receptionist for contractors and add more only when the first one is earning its keep.

Where the hours go

The hidden workday behind every service call

Owners in the trades rarely complain about the work itself. The drain on the week is the stuff wrapped around the work: answering, chasing, confirming, re-confirming and typing the same details into three different places.

Calls you can't take

Hands are full, the truck is loud, a customer is talking to you face to face. Voicemail becomes the front desk, and plenty of homeowners never leave one.

Booking ping-pong

Finding a window that works for the homeowner, the tech and the parts delivery can take four texts and a phone call for one appointment.

Estimates written at night

Measurements and photos from the day get turned into quotes at the kitchen table after dinner, which is when everything feels slowest.

Follow-up that never happens

An estimate goes out, the homeowner goes quiet, and nobody circles back because the next emergency already showed up.

Reviews nobody asks for

Happy customers would gladly leave a review. Most simply never get a polite, well-timed request.

Invoices that age

Finished jobs with unpaid balances sit for weeks because sending reminder number three feels awkward and takes time.

None of these tasks is hard. They are just constant. Our time-back calculator lets you plug in your own numbers and see how many hours a week this kind of work is eating.

Judgment calls

What stays human on your crew

An agent is a helper, not the boss. In a home service business these decisions stay with your people:

  • Pricing and final estimates. The Quote Drafting Agent prepares a draft; a person approves it before the homeowner ever sees a number.
  • Anything that spends money or makes a commitment. Discounts, warranties, change orders and refunds require a human sign-off.
  • Safety calls. Gas, electrical hazards and active leaks follow your escalation rules to a real person, fast.
  • Tough conversations. An unhappy customer or a disputed invoice gets routed to you with the full history attached.
  • The work itself. No agent is climbing your ladder. It just keeps the phone from pulling you off it.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What can an AI receptionist do for a contractor?

It can answer calls in your company's voice, collect the job type and address, check your service area and calendar, book estimates, and text the right person a summary. Emergencies follow escalation rules you set so a human gets them fast.

Will an agent give homeowners prices?

Only if you want it to, and only within rules you approve. Most contractors have the Quote Drafting Agent prepare a draft estimate that a person reviews and approves before it goes to the customer.

Does it work with my field service software?

Agents are designed to connect to the tools you already use, such as your phone system, calendar, CRM, field service platform and QuickBooks. Connections vary by business, so they are scoped in writing after the Agent Blueprint.

How much does an AI agent for a home service business cost?

A Scout starts from $1,500 to build and own or $149 per month plus setup on Subscribe-to-Own. A Specialist such as the AI Receptionist starts from $3,500 or $299 per month plus setup. Deployment is quoted separately after the Blueprint, and usage costs like phone minutes are billed at cost.

Can I start with just one agent?

Yes. Many contractors start with a single AI Receptionist or Speed-to-Lead Responder and add more once the first one is saving time. Bigger squads are deployed in waves.
