For online stores

AI agents for ecommerce teams drowning in "where's my order?"

Running an online store means selling to people around the clock while your support inbox, inventory sheet and review dashboard quietly multiply. AI agents for ecommerce are designed to take the repeatable parts of that load (order lookups, return requests, low-stock alerts, review replies, cart reminders) so your team can focus on products, merchandising and the customers who need a real conversation.

Behind the storefront

Inventory, reviews and the morning report

Inventory Watch

Watches stock levels against thresholds you set, flags fast movers before they run out and drafts a reorder note for your buyer to approve. It doesn't place purchase orders by itself.

Review replies

Drafts on-brand responses to new reviews, publishes the routine thank-yous you pre-approve, and sends anything negative, detailed or legally sensitive to a person with suggested wording.

Daily briefing

A short summary each morning: orders, open tickets, returns in progress, low-stock items and reviews that need your eyes.

These sit in our operations squad and the marketing agents, and they can be added to any support setup later.

Consent first

Abandoned cart follow-up that respects the rules

Cart and browse reminders are useful, but they are marketing messages, and the rules are real. Our Follow-Up Agent is configured to:

  1. Message only people who opted in. Reminders go to shoppers who gave permission through your store's subscription or checkout consent options.
  2. Respect channel rules. Email follows requirements like those in the FTC's CAN-SPAM compliance guide, including honoring opt-outs within 10 business days. Text messages require their own express consent, and rules differ by country, so we review your setup with you.
  3. Keep it short and honest. One or two reminders on a schedule you choose, clear sender identity, and an easy way to stop.
  4. Stop on purchase or opt-out. The moment someone buys or unsubscribes, the sequence ends.
Note: compliance responsibility remains with your business. We build agents to support your consent records, not replace legal advice.

Getting it right

What makes an ecommerce agent trustworthy

An agent answering customers on your behalf is only as good as the data and rules behind it. Before anything goes live, the Agent Blueprint works through the details that separate a helpful agent from an embarrassing one:

One source of truth

Shipping zones, return windows, warranty terms and sizing guides are pulled from your actual policies, not paraphrased from memory. When a policy changes, the agent's knowledge is updated with it.

Real order data

Order and tracking answers come from your store and carrier data through the connections we scope, so customers get their real status instead of a generic estimate.

Clear escalation triggers

Words like "damaged," "allergic," "lawyer" or "chargeback," high order values and repeat contacts route to a person automatically. You choose the triggers.

Your brand voice

Friendly and brief, playful, or formal. Sample replies are reviewed by your team during testing and tuned until they sound like you.

We also test with the messy, real-world questions your support team already sees: the order shipped to an old address, the gift that needs a receipt without prices, the customer who bought two sizes and wants to return one. Agents launch only after they handle those well, and the Agent Care plan keeps tuning them as new patterns show up. If you're running a small store and wondering where to begin, our list of the first five agents a small business should consider is a good primer.

Your team's job

What stays human in your store

  • Money decisions. Refunds, chargebacks, discounts beyond your set limits and purchase orders need a person's approval.
  • Upset customers. Anger, a damaged gift or a safety concern triggers a handoff, not a canned answer.
  • Product and brand calls. What to stock, how to price, what to promise in a sale.
  • Policy exceptions. Only you know when bending a rule wins a customer for life.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is AI customer service for ecommerce?

It is a set of narrow AI agents that answer common questions like order status, shipping and return policy using your real store data, process in-policy requests, and route anything unusual or sensitive to your team.

Can an agent issue refunds?

Only if you allow it, and usually only within limits you set. Most stores have agents send return labels and instructions automatically while refunds, store credit above a threshold and policy exceptions wait for a person.

Is abandoned cart follow-up legal?

It can be when it follows consent and messaging rules. Our Follow-Up Agent is configured to message only shoppers who opted in, include clear sender information and an easy opt-out, and stop when someone buys or unsubscribes. Your business remains responsible for compliance.

Which ecommerce platforms do the agents work with?

Agents are built to connect to the storefront, helpdesk, email and inventory tools you already use. Every connection is confirmed and scoped in writing after the Agent Blueprint.
